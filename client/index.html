ABOUT PROJECT

AI Infinite Cyber City is a lightweight browser-based cyber city game developed as a B.Tech CSE final year project.

The project demonstrates interactive 3D graphics, player movement, mission management, basic AI police behavior, collision boundaries and game-state management.
